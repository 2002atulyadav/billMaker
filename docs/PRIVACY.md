# Dhanzo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

Dhanzo ("the app") is a billing app for small businesses. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version
- **No account and no sign-up.** We don't ask for your phone number, email or password.
- **Your data stays on your phone.** Your business profile, items, customers, clients, bills, payments and settings are saved only in the app's storage on your device. We have **no server**, and we cannot see, collect or sell your data.
- **No ads, no tracking, no analytics.**

## What the app stores, and where
Everything you enter is stored on your phone, in the app's private database:
- business name, owner name, phone, address, logo and UPI ID;
- items and rates;
- customers and clients;
- bills, work entries, payments and reminders sent.

It stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app.

## When information leaves your phone
Only when **you** choose to send it:
- **Sharing a bill or statement** (PDF or image) through the share menu, WhatsApp, email or any app you pick. That app's own privacy policy then applies.
- **Payment reminders** open WhatsApp with a message you can read and edit before sending.
- **Backups** are saved as a file to the place you choose (for example Google Drive, email or WhatsApp). Keep backup files private, because they contain your business data.
- **Voice input** (optional) uses your phone's built-in speech recognition (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone) to turn speech into text. Depending on your phone, the audio may be processed by that service under its own privacy policy. The app only receives the text, and nothing is recorded or stored.

## Permissions the app may ask for
| Permission | Why | When |
|---|---|---|
| Photos | To add your business logo | Only when you tap "Add logo" |
| Microphone and speech recognition | To add bill items by voice | Only when you tap the mic |
| Face ID / fingerprint | To lock the app | Only if you turn on App lock. Checked by your phone; the app never receives your fingerprint or face data |

## Payments
Dhanzo does not process payments. The UPI QR code on a bill opens your customer's own UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, …) to pay **you directly**. Payments you record in Dhanzo are notes you type yourself.

## Children
Dhanzo is meant for business owners and is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting your data
Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored in it. Backup files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.
